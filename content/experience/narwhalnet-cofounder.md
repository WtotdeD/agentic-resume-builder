Co-founded a company that tracked narwhal migration for shipping insurers, on the theory that whatever the narwhals avoided was worth avoiding. Wrote the first version alone, sold it to three insurers before hiring anyone, and grew it to **£1.2M annual recurring revenue** and eleven people before the acquisition.

Did the engineering, the first forty sales calls, and eventually the difficult conversation where the founders admitted the marine biologist had been right about everything.

## Achievements

- **£0 to £1.2M ARR in 29 months** — bootstrapped, no external funding, profitable from month 14
- **Acquired in 2017** — the platform still runs today inside the acquirer
- **Three insurers signed before the first hire** — sold the prototype on its results, not its roadmap

## Community

- **Open-sourced the migration dataset** — eight years of tracking data released publicly; still cited in marine research
- **Founded the Improbability Shire data meetup** — ran monthly for four years and outlived the company

## Leadership & Mentoring

- **Hired and grew eleven people** — including four first engineering jobs, three of whom now lead teams elsewhere
- **Wrote the engineering handbook** — onboarding went from a fortnight of shadowing to two days and a laptop
